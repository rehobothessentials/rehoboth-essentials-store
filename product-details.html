Benefits
▲
  • Deeply nourishes scalp and hair follicles.
  • Reduces hair fall and promotes thick, healthy growth.
  • Adds a natural, non-greasy shine to dull hair.
  • Soothes irritated scalp with cooling herbs.
Key Ingredients
▼

Virgin Coconut Oil, Henna, Black Cumin, Fenugreek, Curry Leaves, Amla, Brahmi, and traditional Ayurvedic herbs.

How to Use
▼
  1. Warm the oil slightly (optional) for better absorption.
  2. Apply generously to the scalp and hair roots.
  3. Massage gently for 10-15 minutes in circular motions.
  4. Leave for at least 2-3 hours or overnight for deep nourishment.
  5. Wash off with a mild shampoo and lukewarm water.
  6. Use 2-3 times a week for best results.
Storage & Care
▼
  • Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
  • Keep away from excessive heat and moisture.
  • Close the cap tightly after each use.
  • Use within 12 months from the date of opening.
  • Do not refrigerate.
Suitable For
▼
  • All hair types (dry, oily, combination)
  • Sensitive and irritated scalps
  • Hair prone to breakage and hair fall
  • Dull and lifeless hair
  • Men and women of all ages
Important Info
▼
  • For external use only.
  • Discontinue use if irritation occurs. Perform a patch test before full application.
  • Keep away from children and pets.
  • Avoid contact with eyes. In case of contact, rinse immediately with water.
  • Consult a dermatologist if you have a specific scalp condition.
  • This product is not tested on animals and is cruelty-free.
